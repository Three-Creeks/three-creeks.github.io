The Plan

Integrated destination resort. 50-lot cabin community. Five-year build-out, fully funded by the program itself.

The 60-acre developable portion of the Property supports a phased 50-lot luxury cabin community. The community is the development product. The venue is the demand engine. The Highland cattle, chapel, and 119-acre setting are the brand. Together they constitute the first fully-integrated luxury wedding-and-resort destination in the Blue Ridge market.

Three Revenue Channels, One Asset

Each cabin generates revenue through all three of the following channels:

  • For-Sale Inventory — 50 cabins sold over 5 years at $725K–$895K, generating gross sales of approximately $40M and funding retirement of the Estate Note
  • Short-Term Rental Inventory — Cabins held during pre-sale at $58K–$120K/yr per cabin per AirDNA Blue Ridge market data
  • Venue-Tied Full-Resort Weekends — Wedding weekends at $25K–$45K per event including cabin block buyouts, doubling the venue's per-event revenue

Why the Estate Recovery Floor Beats the Cash Alternative

Even in a default scenario, the Estate's recovery floor under this transaction exceeds the nominal recovery of any all-cash distressed alternative, because:

  • The Estate holds a first-position mortgage on improved real estate worth materially more than the Note balance throughout the term
  • The Property's collateral value is contractually positioned to grow during the Note term as the cabin community is developed
  • The Estate holds a personal guarantee from a principal with $24B+ in successful project delivery and a clean credit record
  • $1.485M cash plus interim payments are received and irreversible before any default event could materialize

Carrying-Cost Erosion of the Alternatives

Each additional month the Property remains in Estate custody costs $31,000–$50,000 in documentable carrying costs: commercial insurance, security, livestock husbandry, receiver and counsel fees, property taxes, and utilities. Continued marketing imposes a real, ongoing cost that this transaction terminates within 35 days.
